The earlier broad claims “Mailings absent”, “Review translate absent”, or “AI cannot review/edit notes” are incorrect for the recent source. Local CSV single-document merge, 22-language Editor/Translate presets, markup/filter/body decision scopes, comments lifecycle, four protection settings, readonly review, and authorized AI note/review candidates all have handlers. Manual UI capabilities and AI-exposed capabilities are separate dimensions.

Intentional host constraints: NativeAI freezes exact source/object hashes and grants, previews candidates and requires explicit adoption, limits writes to 100 operations/targets, refuses unsupported source structures, isolates comment/revision/protection/merge families and often requires save/reopen. These are policy boundaries rather than bugs. They do not imply source-equivalent operations exist. Protected documents are deliberately wholly readonly locally in every enforced mode, though all four modes serialize correctly.
